## What they do instead

Mostly **downward to free**, occasionally sideways. "Going to switch tools because this one obviously doesn't have the upkeep I would expect for a video editing tool I am paying for" (CapCut, 2026-11-09). "I can do that setting on TikTok platform for free" was reported by the round-1 assistant but falls outside the verified window and is **not carried forward**.

## What they say they would pay for

Only one thing is evidenced, and it is **control**, not intelligence: an off switch per automated behaviour, and a watermark that does not appear after the work. Nobody in the verified sample asks for style-matching. **Treat "would pay for style-matching" as unproven.**
